Q: Why do you want to study in the UK?
A: I chose to study in the United Kingdom because it is internationally recognised for its high-quality education, strong academic standards, and practical approach to learning.
UK universities focus on research, critical thinking, teamwork, and the practical application of knowledge through case studies, presentations, and real business projects. Another important reason is that a UK master's degree can be completed in just one year, allowing me to gain a globally recognised qualification while saving both time and overall study costs.
I believe studying in the UK will enhance my professional knowledge, improve my global business understanding, and strengthen my career opportunities when I return to India.
